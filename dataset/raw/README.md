# Real training videos

Add controlled MP4, MOV, M4V, WEBM, AVI, or MKV exercise recordings to the
matching exercise directory:

- `squat/`
- `single_leg_hop/`
- `single_leg_balance/`

For every video, add one row to `../labels.csv`. `video_id` must be the video
filename, including its extension. Labels must be assigned by a suitably
qualified clinician or under an approved research protocol; do not infer a
return-to-play label from the video alone.
